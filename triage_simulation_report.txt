5. CONCEPTUAL MAPPINGS & COMPLEXITY OVERVIEW
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Selection Sort: O(n^2) worst/average time, O(1) space. Chosen for small queues to avoid recursion overhead.
- Merge Sort: O(n log n) best/worst/average time, O(n) space. Scalable for large disaster triage queues.
- Process Modeling: Patients act as active processes. PID = patient ID, Burst Time = treatment duration, Arrival Time = check-in.
- Preemptive Priority: Higher priority triage score immediately preempts/interrupts current treatment if a doctor is busy.
- Round Robin: Ensures fairness. Doctors treat in intervals (Quantum=5m), guaranteeing lower priority starvation prevention.
- Aging Mechanism: Automatically increments priority scores of patients waiting past threshold, preventing infinite starvation.
